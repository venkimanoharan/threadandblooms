A Gift That Never Wilts

Real flowers fade in a week. A handcrafted crochet bouquet from Thread and Blooms lasts a lifetime — and it's made to order right here in San Antonio, Texas. Here's when (and why) our customers order most.

Valentine's Day 🌹

Our red rose bouquets are, unsurprisingly, our most-requested design every February. Unlike cut roses, a crochet bouquet is still on display next Valentine's Day — and the one after that. Valentine's is our busiest season, so the earlier you reach out, the more likely we can fit your order in before the 14th.

Order for Valentine's Day

Mother's Day 🌸

Soft pastels and delicate florals make for a keepsake gift Mom can display year-round, not just until the vase water runs out. A handcrafted bouquet says you put real thought into it — because you did. Mother's Day is another peak season for us, so we recommend ordering a few weeks ahead.

Order for Mother's Day

Christmas & The Holidays 🎄

A crochet bouquet makes a warm, personal gift under the tree — something that won't get lost in the usual pile of gift cards. Great for moms, grandmothers, teachers, and anyone who'd rather have something handmade than store-bought. December books up fast, so early orders get first pick of availability.
