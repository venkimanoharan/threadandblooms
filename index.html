Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you order

How long does it take to receive my order?

Most bouquets are handcrafted and ready within 1–2 weeks of confirming your order, depending on size and current order volume.

Do you ship outside San Antonio?

Right now, Thread and Blooms is local pickup and local delivery only within San Antonio, Texas. We don't currently ship nationwide — message us to check delivery availability in your area.

How much does a bouquet cost?

Pricing starts at $50 for a 2-foot bouquet and $120 for a 4-foot bouquet, varying based on flower selection and customization. Message us for an exact quote for your design.

Can I customize the colors and flowers?

Yes! Every bouquet is made to order. Share your color palette, occasion, and any special requests, and we'll design something one-of-a-kind for you.

How do I care for my crochet bouquet?

Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from moisture to preserve the yarn colors. Spot clean gently with a damp cloth — these are decorative pieces made to be treasured for years, not real flowers that wilt.

How do I place an order?

Tap "Order Now" to message us on WhatsApp or text, or reach out on Instagram @threadandbloom.satx. We'll walk you through sizing, flower options, and timeline.
